**OPSWAT FileScan** Standalone Edition is the standalone software appliance of the popular **OPSWAT FileScan** - Next-Gen Malware Analysis Platform community service that allows rapid and in-depth dynamic analysis of massive amounts of files, documents, archives and URLs. It is a **next-gen sandbox that runs cloud-native** at 10x speed of traditional sandboxes with 90% less resource usage, cloud-native or on premise (including air gapped). In this user guide, software requirements, an installation guide and extensive outlines of all setup and configuration options will be presented.
