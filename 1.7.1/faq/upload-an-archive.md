Yes, a wide range of archives is supported (zip, gzip, tar, wim, 7z, rar, etc.). If the archive contains multiple files, all embedded files are analyzed.

More details about arhives are available [here](https://docs.opswat.com/filescan/datasheet/supported-file-types).
