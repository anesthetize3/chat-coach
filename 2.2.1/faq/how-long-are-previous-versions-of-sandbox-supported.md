We maintain backward compatibility within each major version of MetaDefender Sandbox. However, due to the evolving nature of the threat landscape, we recommend that users upgrade to the latest version to ensure they benefit from the most up-to-date features, improvements, and security enhancements. **Our release cycle typically spans 90 days, with each version designed to work seamlessly within its respective major release.**
