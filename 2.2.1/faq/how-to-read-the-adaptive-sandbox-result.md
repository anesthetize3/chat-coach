**IOC (Indicators of Compromise)**

{% image url="https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/XX2D/h0na8lrqm62ey3cczpqslyglpl7dmjwzmu0f3pdeee2koos853alh8pdlcvserk9.png" /%}

An Indicator of Compromise (IoC) is a piece of digital forensic evidence that suggests a potential breach of a network or endpoint system. These indicators serve as critical clues that help cybersecurity professionals identify malicious activities, including data breaches, malware infections, and insider threats. By the time an IoC is detected, it typically indicates that an attack has already occurred, which allows security teams to take action to mitigate damage and understand the nature of the breach.
