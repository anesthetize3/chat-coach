- Ubuntu 20.04 LTS
- 2 GHz dual core processor or better
- 4 GB system memory or more
- 25 GB of free hard drive space
- Internet access is helpful for a variety of integrations
- Intel or AMD based chipset on the host system

*Note: please refer to the sizing calculator (“FileScanIO\_Sizing\_Calculator.xlsx”) to get a better understanding of how different hardware setups can impact the throughput and allow scaling the overall system.*
