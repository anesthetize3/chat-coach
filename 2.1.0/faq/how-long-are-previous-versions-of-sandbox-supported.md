MetaDefender Sandbox (Standalone) operates under a rolling support model, ensuring that the three most recent versions remain available for download on the myOPSWAT Portal. These versions receive support for critical vulnerabilities, meaning End-of-Life (EOL) in a strict sense does not apply. However, new features and enhancements are only added to the latest version.

We follow a typical release cadence of 90 days, ensuring frequent updates to provide the latest capabilities and maintain security. Customers are encouraged to upgrade to the newest version to take full advantage of improvements and optimizations.
