The final reporting will have a verdict based on a confidence level and threat score. In general, if no confidence in the analysis result is present (e.g. due to fatal errors), the verdict will default to the "UNKNOWN" verdict. If there is sufficient confidence, a regular verdict will be generated. Here is an overview of the default palette of verdicts and their color representations:
