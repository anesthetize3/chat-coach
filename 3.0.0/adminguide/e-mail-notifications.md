### Step 1- Sign-up

You need to click on "**Sign in**" first in the right corner of the page in order to choose the "**Sign up now**" function.

{% image url="../../assets/505ad54db9b6ebf48670ac84ce071371b77a1058.png" /%}

Now you may click on the "**Sign up now**"

{% image url="../../assets/8a254f99005fe113d4397f63e3449f972c561060.png" /%}

### Step 2 - Register

Start your registration under the "**Sign-up"** page and insert your email and desired password for future logins.

{% callout title="Info" %}
By registering on MetaDefender Aether, you will automatically receive **USER** rights. *See more details on this page.*
{% /callout %}

{% badge text="Required" type="error" /%} Agreement to the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use are required.

{% image url="../../assets/7cc77e7cdd05238545ed5c1dc3cdc9d06711095a.png" /%}

### Step 3 - Confirm your email

Afterwards, you will receive an email to your email address with a confirmation link to verify your account.

{% image url="../../assets/897875ad41de1884b9f2a0a4a28da230d3f727ec.png" /%}

Please click on the "**Confirm your email**" link

{% image url="https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/XX2D/0yh97lvwtszyzyh3ps78ldtj8zrisnhjqovjcfdejlb1ptj6f53z2io0ces75xea.png" /%}

It will direct you back to the **Homepage** of MetaDefender Aether

{% image url="../../assets/86fb3969c89ba010acf191ecaa73701e7b3eaddb.png" /%}
