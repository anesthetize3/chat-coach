### Step 1- Sign-up

You need to click on "**Sign in**" first in the right corner of the page in order to choose the "**Sign up now**" function.

Now you may click on the "**Sign up now**"

{% image url="https://uploads.developerhub.io/prod/XX2D/oyjoxnff5ukwyi9seh09yebu2lx93osegn3r1dannttsk5aezelb954abk6vvh1y.png" /%}

### Step 2 - Register

Start your registration under the "**Sign-up"** page and insert your email and desired password for future logins.

{% callout title="Info" %}
By registering on Filescan.io, you will automatically receive **USER** rights. *See more details on this page.*
{% /callout %}

{% badge text="Required" type="error" /%} Agreement to the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use are required.
