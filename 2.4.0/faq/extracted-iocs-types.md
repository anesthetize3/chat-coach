All extracted IOCs are listed in the "Indicators of Compromise" section of a report, separated by the artifact from which they were extracted. The range of data types includes hashes, IP addresses, URLs, Domains, Registry Pathways, UUIDs (e.g. "Revision IDs" in an Office document) and are fully supported by our advanced search feature.
