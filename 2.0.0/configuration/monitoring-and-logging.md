At the **Admin Panel \> Setting \> Configurations** section, various settings related to jobs, health checks, and audits can be configured.

- [Jobs](monitoring-and-logging/jobs.md) settings, where e.g.: retry attempts on failure, maximum rescan limit, and storage-related settings can be configured.
- [Healthcheck](monitoring-and-logging/healthcheck.md) settings, such as healthcheck period, healthchecks URI, or queue healthcheck configurations
- [Audit Logging for Admin Settings and User Authentication](monitoring-and-logging/audit-logger.md), where audit logs can be enabled or disables
